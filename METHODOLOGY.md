# SolitudeLabs Archive Methodology

## Purpose

This repository preserves traceable records that support SolitudeLabs publications. It is not a claim of formal archaeological excavation or institutional investigative authority. “Urban archaeology” is used as an approach to reading present-day places through names, signs, maps, directories, dated imagery, documents, and local memory.

## Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| VERIFIED | Directly supported by an identified primary or authoritative source, within the scope stated. |
| CORROBORATED | Supported by more than one independent source. |
| REPORTED | Stated by a secondary source or recollection, not independently confirmed. |
| INFERENCE | An interpretation drawn from available evidence; not itself a directly observed fact. |
| UNKNOWN | The available evidence does not answer the question. |
| NOT VERIFIED | A claim or detail has not yet been checked adequately. |

A label applies only to the specific claim and scope it accompanies. A directory listing does not prove current operation. A street number does not by itself identify a building across different dates. Geographic proximity does not establish ownership, affiliation, or historical connection.

## Source handling

1. Prefer primary documents, official records, direct signage, and dated imagery where available.
2. Record source title, publisher, date, URL, and access date when known.
3. Keep conflicting records visible; do not silently select the convenient version.
4. Separate observation, testimony, secondary reporting, and interpretation.
5. State what would be needed to resolve an uncertainty.

## Corrections

Archive records may be revised when new evidence appears. Corrections should preserve the reason for the change and avoid presenting an unresolved question as settled.

## Scope note

The Markdown records in this repository are archive summaries and metadata, not verbatim mirrors of the full WordPress articles. The publication links are the canonical article locations.
