**Publication:** [SolitudeLabs — WordPress](https://solitudelabs.wordpress.com/2026/11/28/the-globalisation-tapes-serikat-buruh-pembuat-film-dan-jejak-menuju-the-act-of-killing/)

**Scope:** The Globalisation Tapes (2003), IUF, SERBUK, Hemasari Dharmabumi, Joshua Oppenheimer, and the research trajectory toward The Act of Killing.

## Summary

This record preserves the documentary claims and caveats in the published SolitudeLabs article. Joshua Oppenheimer's 2004 dissertation, *Show of Force: Film, Ghosts and Genres of Historical Performance in the Indonesian Genocide*, describes three distinct agendas around *The Globalisation Tapes*: those of the filmmakers, an international agricultural workers' union, and SERBUK. It describes contact from an unnamed female IUF labour educator and IUF Indonesia's recommendation to work with SERBUK in plantation villages in Deli-Serdang, North Sumatra.

The dissertation's *Dramatis Personae* names five key plantation-worker filmmakers: Sukirman, Eddraman, G Siahaan, Astaman, and Ibu Ati. Footnote 52 lists twelve principal SERBUK filmmakers involved in shooting and editing. The source spells the Siahaan initial differently in the two locations (G Siahaan / R. Siahaan); this discrepancy is retained and not silently reconciled.

Contemporaneous and institutional records identify Hemasari Dharmabumi as Labour Education Centre coordinator and IUF representative in Indonesia in 2000. This does **not** establish that she was the unnamed woman who contacted Oppenheimer. No direct Hemasari-to-Oppenheimer contact edge is asserted.

The available records support IUF and SERBUK involvement in the project, but do not establish that IUF or APHEDA financed the film. APHEDA funding of the Labour Education Centre must not be converted into a claim that APHEDA funded *The Globalisation Tapes*. The relationship between *The Globalisation Tapes* and *The Act of Killing* is treated as a research/creative trajectory, not proof of a shared production or funding structure.

## Methodological note

A role is not proof of a specific action. Institutional proximity is not proof of communication. Participation is not proof of financing. “Not verified” means that the evidence reviewed does not establish the claim; it does not prove that the event never occurred.
