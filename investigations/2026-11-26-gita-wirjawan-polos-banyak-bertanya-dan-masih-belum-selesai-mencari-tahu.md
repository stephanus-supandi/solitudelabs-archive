## Scope and summary

A light parody of the *Endgame* podcast format, using Gita Wirjawan's conversation with education practitioner Antarina SF Amir as its point of departure. The article jokes that a podcast host does not need to be an expert in every subject: the host's role is to invite knowledgeable guests, ask relevant questions, listen, and help the audience follow a discussion.

The episode topics described in the article include educational philosophy, Ki Hadjar Dewantara, teacher recruitment and training, teacher welfare, PISA, parents, and the relationship between politics and education policy. The piece uses “polos” as a satirical description of the host's questioning persona, while explicitly distinguishing that joke from the much stronger factual claim that he “knows nothing.” It states that one episode cannot establish the full extent of a person's knowledge or intellectual ability.

The article also cautions readers not to treat a guest's explanation as automatically conclusive. Claims about PISA should be checked against the data; claims about teachers against evidence on teacher conditions; and claims about policy against the relevant policy documents and research.
