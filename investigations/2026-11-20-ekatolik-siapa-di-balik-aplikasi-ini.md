# eKatolik: Siapa di Balik Aplikasi Ini?

## Editorial scope

An initial public-source investigation into the origin, people, development history, funding disclosures, and institutional relationships of the eKatolik application. The article asks who created and maintained the service, what financial support has been publicly described, and whether references to Catholic institutions indicate content sourcing, permission, cooperation, sponsorship, or formal ownership.

## Article summary

The article identifies Bernardus Agus Cahyono as the creator/developer described in a 19 October 2017 HIDUPKATOLIK profile. That profile dates the start of Android development to September 2013 and the launch of eKatolik to 6 November 2013. It also names Stefan Kasih, Taufan, and Christian Xun as collaborators in the development period described by the 2017 article.

The same historical profile reports that an unnamed donor provided funds around October 2016 to resume development after a period of reduced activity. The donor's identity, contribution amount, terms, and any continuing role are not disclosed in the source cited by the article.

The article compares this historical account with current platform metadata supplied by Google Play and Apple App Store. Google Play displays Infinitech Indonesia as the developer label and also lists Bernados Agus Cahyono in developer contact information; Apple's App Store listing names Bernados Agus Cahyono as developer and seller. The spelling difference between “Bernardus” in the 2017 profile and “Bernados” in platform metadata is recorded without assuming either a second person or a confirmed clerical error. The legal status and ownership structure of Infinitech Indonesia are not established by the app-store labels alone.

The article records publicly displayed “Persembahan Kasih” support information and the historical mention of religious advertising. It does not infer total revenue, current donor identities, operating costs, or the use of donations because no public financial statement was identified in the reviewed material.

The application listing refers to content sources or permissions associated with LAI, the KWI Commission for Liturgy, and Penerbit Obor. The article carefully distinguishes content sourcing and copyright permission from formal ownership, governance, sponsorship, or financial control. It does not claim that eKatolik is an official KWI or diocesan application.

## Open questions

1. What is the legal and operational status of “Infinitech Indonesia”?
2. Who currently maintains the application and its content?
3. Who was the donor mentioned in the 2017 profile, and what were the amount and terms of the 2016 support?
4. What are the present sources of operating revenue and the destination of user contributions?
5. Are there written agreements with KWI, LAI, Penerbit Obor, or any diocese, and what is their precise scope?
6. Is there a formal institutional endorsement or ownership relationship beyond the content references visible in app-store metadata?

## Method note

Distinguish historical reporting, current platform metadata, content licensing, editorial cooperation, financial support, and formal institutional authority. Do not infer ownership from an app-store developer label, sponsorship from the use of liturgical materials, or a donor's identity from circumstantial association. Treat unknown funding details as unknown rather than suspicious. This record summarizes the published article; it is not a byte-for-byte mirror.
