## Subject

A popular-history article about Trish Vickers, a blind woman in Dorset, England, who wrote 26 pages of her first novel with a pen that had run out of ink. The article describes how pressure marks left on the paper enabled forensic recovery of much of the writing.

## Article structure

1. The 26 apparently blank pages.
2. Pressure marks left by the pen and the forensic recovery.

4. The Zack D. Films Shorts as a retelling/visualization, not original 2012 footage.
