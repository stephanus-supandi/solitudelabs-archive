## Editorial summary

The article examines the Gedung Karya Pastoral (GKP) of Gereja Santo Bartolomeus (Sanbarto), Taman Galaxi, as a pastoral and community facility rather than treating it only as a church meeting building. Parish publications describe the GKP as a place for pastoral services, meetings, catechetical and group activities, community interaction, a multipurpose hall, and social services. The 20th-anniversary booklet records that construction followed a permit dated 26 June 2003 and that the building was first used on 24 September 2006. A job fair was held there on 22 November 2014; a health clinic and other community-facing services are also documented in parish material.

A specifically documented interfaith event took place at the GKP on 9 November 2024 for the International Day for Tolerance, with the theme “Kota Bekasi Kota Toleran di Indonesia.” Sanbarto's 2025 Christmas publication places the event on the GKP's third floor and identifies participation by the Bekasi FKUB chair, local government representatives, leaders or representatives of six religious communities, neighborhood figures, parish representatives, and the Forum Komunikasi Gereja-gereja se-Galaxi. The event was organized collaboratively by FKGG, the parish's HAAK section, and the 2024 Christmas committee.

The article keeps separate events separate. A cross-faith prayer and declaration opposing radicalism and terrorism was reported on 19 May 2018 in the church's parking courtyard; the cited report does not establish that it took place inside the GKP. A 2019 profile describes routine neighborly relations such as halal bihalal, participation around Idul Adha, and community assistance. Public officials also attended distinct Sanbarto events in 2025 and 2026, including anniversary-related and community activities; those appearances are not automatically evidence that each official attended the 2024 interfaith dialogue.

The article also asks who works in or manages the GKP, but does not claim to have established a complete staff or management roster. Parish publications contain names in different capacities, including editorial, parish-council, and legal-consultation contexts. A person's name, by itself, does not establish ethnicity, employment status, job function, or authority. The available sources do not establish the ethnic composition of GKP employees, volunteers, administrators, or pastoral leadership. These roles must be distinguished and verified through an actual roster, appointment records, or direct institutional confirmation.

## Evidence boundaries

- **Documented in parish publications:** GKP use from 24 September 2006; pastoral/community functions; the 9 November 2024 tolerance event at the GKP; and other separately dated parish or community activities.
- **Reported by external media:** Sanbarto's everyday interfaith and neighborhood activities in the 2019 HIDUP Katolik profile; the 19 May 2018 cross-faith prayer/declaration at the church courtyard.
- **Not established:** A single continuous event linking all named public officials; that the 2018 courtyard event occurred inside the GKP; a complete current GKP staffing or management structure; or the ethnic identity/composition of staff and administrators.
- **Methodological warning:** Do not infer ethnicity from names, appearance, or presumed ancestry. Do not equate a parish council member, publication editor, volunteer, clergy member, legal-service team member, and paid GKP employee.
