## Archive summary

A manifesto for Ensiklopedia BLOON as an independent, personal editorial and knowledge project. It is not Wikipedia and does not claim institutional encyclopedic authority. Its purpose is to document claims, memories, hypotheses, uncertainties, and research status transparently.

The article establishes the working motto:

> GOBLOK BOLEH. NGARANG JANGAN.

It proposes evidence-status labels including VERIFIED, PARTIALLY VERIFIED, PERSONAL MEMORY, HYPOTHESIS, NOT VERIFIED, NOT FOUND, and NOT RUN. These labels must be applied to individual claims rather than used as blanket judgments about an entire subject.

## Editorial rules preserved

- Separate documented facts, personal recollection, inference, hypothesis, and unknowns.
- Absence of evidence is not automatically evidence of absence.
- Do not force unrelated articles into one storyline.
- Similarity of names, themes, or patterns does not prove identity, ownership, causation, or historical connection.
- Treat the remembered Circle K outlet near the present-day BNI area in Taman Galaxy, circa 1996–2000, as **PERSONAL MEMORY / NOT VERIFIED**, not as an established historical fact.
