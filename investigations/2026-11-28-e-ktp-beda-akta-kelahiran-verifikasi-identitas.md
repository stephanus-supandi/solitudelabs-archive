## Summary

The article examines what it means when information on an Indonesian electronic identity card (e-KTP) differs from a birth certificate. It distinguishes the civil-registration function of a birth certificate from the population-identity function of an e-KTP, and argues that a discrepancy should be investigated without automatically treating it as evidence of identity fraud.

Four possible explanations are identified:

1. A clerical or data-entry error.
2. A lawful name change supported by the required legal and civil-registration records.
3. Administrative inconsistency between older documents, newer documents, and the population-administration database.
4. Possible identity-data manipulation, which requires evidence and official examination.

The article highlights the legal process for recording a name change, including the role of a District Court determination under Article 52 of Law No. 23 of 2006 on Population Administration. It also explains that an NIK is intended to be unique and lifelong, so a name change does not by itself establish that a person has acquired an entirely new population identity.

The recommended verification path is to compare lawfully obtained original documents, identify exactly which data fields differ, check the legal basis and administrative history of any change, request clarification from the competent Disdukcapil, and use formal complaint or law-enforcement channels if there is credible evidence of misuse.

## Editorial and evidence boundaries

- A mismatch between two documents is a reason to verify, not proof of criminal conduct.
- A lawful name change, a clerical error, an administrative synchronization issue, and deliberate manipulation are distinct possibilities.
- The article does not accuse any named individual or group of identity fraud.
- Personal identifiers such as NIK and images of identity documents must not be exposed or circulated casually.
- Any individual case remains **NOT VERIFIED** unless supported by lawfully obtained records and competent examination.
