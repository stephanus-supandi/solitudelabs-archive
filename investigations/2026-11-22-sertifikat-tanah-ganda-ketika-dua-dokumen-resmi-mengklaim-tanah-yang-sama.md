## Ringkasan arsip

Examines the legal and administrative problem of two land certificates claiming the same parcel. The article explains the evidentiary role of land certificates under Indonesia's land-registration framework; distinguishes overlapping physical data, unclear chains of title, false documents, and abuse of authority as different possible issues; outlines PPAT's role and professional duties; and discusses the Supreme Court's reported jurisprudential position on competing authentic certificates. It emphasizes that the earlier-issued certificate may carry stronger evidentiary weight in the described circumstances, but the outcome cannot be reduced to comparing dates alone: the parcel identity, legal basis, registration history, administrative process, and facts of the case must still be examined.

The article also distinguishes administrative error, civil land disputes, and criminal conduct. It does not presume that BPN, PPAT, village officials, or any party is culpable merely because a dispute exists. It identifies records that may be relevant to an evidence-based complaint and frames public accountability around verifiable documents and the limits of each institution's authority.

## Pokok bahasan

1. Sertifikat sebagai alat bukti kuat atas data fisik dan yuridis, bukan dokumen yang kebal dari pemeriksaan hukum.
2. Kemungkinan sumber persoalan: tumpang tindih data bidang, riwayat peralihan hak yang tidak jelas, dokumen atau keterangan palsu, dan dugaan penyalahgunaan kewenangan.
3. Kewenangan dan kewajiban profesional PPAT berdasarkan PP Nomor 37 Tahun 1998 sebagaimana diubah dengan PP Nomor 24 Tahun 2016.
4. Pemberitaan Mahkamah Agung tanggal 5 April 2025 mengenai yurisprudensi sertifikat tanah ganda; prinsip sertifikat yang terbit lebih dahulu tidak diperlakukan sebagai rumus otomatis untuk semua perkara.
5. Pembedaan tanggung jawab pemegang hak, PPAT, Kantor Pertanahan/BPN, pemerintah desa atau kelurahan, dan aparat penegak hukum.
6. Perbedaan sengketa pertanahan, kesalahan administrasi, pemalsuan, dan tindak pidana.
7. Pentingnya memeriksa riwayat penerbitan, objek bidang, hasil pengukuran, alas hak, transaksi, prosedur administrasi, dan putusan atau keputusan yang relevan.

## Batas kesimpulan dan catatan metodologi

- This is a general legal and policy discussion, not legal advice on a specific parcel or dispute.
- The article does not establish that any named person, PPAT, notary, BPN office, village official, or institution committed wrongdoing.
- The reported "earlier certificate" principle must be read in the context of the relevant jurisprudence and case facts; do not generalize it into an unconditional rule that the oldest certificate always wins.
