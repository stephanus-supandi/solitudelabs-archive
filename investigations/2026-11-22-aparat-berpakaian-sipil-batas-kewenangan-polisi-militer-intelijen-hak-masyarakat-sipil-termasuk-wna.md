## Summary

Mengkaji batas kewenangan aparat yang bertugas dengan pakaian sipil, termasuk polisi, TNI, fungsi intelijen, Polisi Militer, dan petugas keimigrasian. Membahas perbedaan penyelidikan, permintaan keterangan, penangkapan, dan penahanan; ketentuan penangkapan dalam KUHAP baru UU 20/2025, termasuk pengecualian tertangkap tangan; serta prinsip legalitas, kebutuhan, proporsionalitas, dan akuntabilitas dalam penggunaan kekuatan. Bagian khusus WNA membedakan proses pidana dari tindakan administratif keimigrasian dan menjelaskan perlindungan dasar serta hak komunikasi konsuler berdasarkan Konvensi Wina 1963. Artikel menekankan bahwa pakaian sipil atau klaim sebagai intel tidak dengan sendirinya membuktikan tindakan ilegal maupun memberikan kewenangan tanpa batas. Tidak menilai kasus atau individu tertentu.

## Scope

- Plainclothes and undercover activity: distinguish lawful covert observation or investigation from coercive actions that require separate legal authority.
- Police powers: distinguish investigation, questioning, arrest, detention, and other restrictions on liberty.
- Arrest procedure: discuss UU 20/2025 (new KUHAP), the general warrant procedure, the caught-in-the-act exception, and prompt handover to an investigator.
- Use of force: assess legality, necessity, proportionality, minimal harm, and accountability; do not treat official status as blanket permission to use violence.
- Police and military: distinguish Polri, TNI, military intelligence, Polisi Militer, and legally assigned assistance to Polri.
- Foreign nationals: distinguish criminal proceedings from immigration administrative measures; cover baseline human-rights protections and consular communication under Article 36 of the Vienna Convention on Consular Relations 1963.
- Complaints and safeguards: identify possible channels such as Propam, Polisi Militer, Komnas HAM, legal counsel, and consular representatives, depending on the facts and institution involved.

## Evidence boundaries and open questions

1. This is a general legal explainer, not a finding that a particular officer or institution committed misconduct.
2. Wearing civilian clothes does not, by itself, establish illegality; nor does a claim to be an intelligence officer prove authority to arrest or use force.
3. The legal distinction between arrest, detention, voluntary questioning, immigration inspection, and administrative immigration action must be preserved.
