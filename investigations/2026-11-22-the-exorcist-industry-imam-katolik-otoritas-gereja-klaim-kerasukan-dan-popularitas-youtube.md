## Ringkasan arsip

Investigates the visibility of Catholic exorcism content on YouTube and the distinction between priestly status, public commentary on demonology, past or present pastoral experience, and a specific authorization to perform a major exorcism. Reviews Canon 1172, USCCB guidance, and five media-visible figures: Chad Ripperger, Carlos Martins, Dan Reehil, Stephen Rossetti, and Gabriele Amorth. It also considers the media debate over exorcists in the spotlight, an Indonesian press report referring to an anonymized priest as Father Budi, and the need for medical and mental-health assessment. It does not diagnose people from videos or certify supernatural claims.

1. Eksorsisme sebagai topik konten digital dan pertanyaan tentang popularitas versus kewenangan.

3. Lima tokoh yang dibahas: Chad Ripperger, Carlos Martins, Dan Reehil, Stephen Rossetti, dan Gabriele Amorth.
4. Perdebatan media mengenai imam atau exorcist yang tampil di ruang publik.
5. Perbedaan antara klaim kerasukan, gejala medis atau psikologis, dan penilaian gerejawi.
6. Pemberitaan Indonesia tahun 2017 yang menggunakan nama samaran Father Budi; identitas asli tidak ditebak.
7. Daftar pertanyaan verifikasi: identitas, status, institusi, penugasan, pernyataan, dokumentasi, konteks, dan pengawasan.

## Batas kesimpulan dan catatan metodologi

- Public documentation does not establish every current or historical diocesan appointment discussed; where no public appointment record is available, status must remain unverified independently.
- The anonymized identity in the 2017 VICE report is not inferred or reconstructed.
- The existence of an exorcism rite does not establish that any specific reported case was supernatural; individual cases require independent and appropriate evidence.
