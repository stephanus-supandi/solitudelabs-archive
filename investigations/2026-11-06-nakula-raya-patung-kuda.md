## Findings and limits

The article combines map/directory references, dated visual observations, and local exploration. These sources have different evidentiary weight and should not be treated as interchangeable.

The discrepancy between building numbers 211 and 487 on Jalan Bumi is unresolved. Without matching physical features and dates, it is not safe to conclude that the references identify the same building or document an address change.

The article also notes a source discrepancy around the inauguration of Taman Gigi: some accounts refer to PDGI Kota Bekasi, while an ANTARA account refers to IDI. Those references should remain distinct unless primary documentation resolves the difference.

A directory listing for Bakmi Ayam Olim is not enough to establish its founding date or founder. A secondary account about another mie ayam business is not proof of Olim's history.
