## Editorial summary

The article compares two distinct settlement histories within Kebayoran Baru. Petogogan is discussed through research describing workers' barracks and later government/PDAM-related residents during the 1950s development period. Kampung Leuser is discussed as an informal settlement in an area planned as part of the eastern greenbelt, with residents' reported claim of occupation since 1955 and a history associated with PAM Jaya facilities.

The article distinguishes residents' oral histories and claims, physical occupation, land-use planning, land-registration documents, and legal ownership. It discusses the 2012 HGB registered to PAM Jaya and the 2016 dispute described in academic research, while avoiding any conclusion about the present legal status or the ultimate validity of competing claims. It emphasizes that the two settlements do not share one origin story and that urban planning, workers' housing, informal settlement, and land disputes must be examined separately.

## Evidence boundaries and cautions

- The 1955 settlement chronology for Petogogan is attributed to a resident's account reported in 2013 and academic research; it is not presented as a complete cadastral history.
- The Kampung Leuser settlement date is described as a claim reported by residents in research, not as proof of registered ownership.
- The HGB and dispute chronology are reported from the cited academic study. This archive entry does not establish the legal status after the study's coverage period.
- Long-term occupation, tax payment, a permit to reside, and a registered land right are distinct matters.
- Do not generalize either settlement's history to all residents or infer ethnicity, ownership, or political affiliation from location or business presence.
