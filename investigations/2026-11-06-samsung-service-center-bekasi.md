## Research question

Where was the Samsung service-center location remembered from an earlier visit, and can that memory be matched to a current or historical listing?

## Findings and limits

The search identified directory references to a Samsung Service Center at Mega Bekasi Hypermall, Jalan Jenderal Ahmad Yani No. 1. Published listings disagree about the unit number:

- Some Samsung-related directory/document references list floor 1, unit 167-168.
- Other outlet listings/documents list unit 176-177.

The discrepancy is recorded rather than silently resolved. It may reflect different listings, service versus retail locations, or a change over time; the available information does not establish which explanation is correct.

The article also discusses the mall's history and Samsung after-sales support, including Maintenance Mode. These contextual details do not establish that the listed service center is currently operating at the stated unit.

The remembered connection to Giant is an unverified recollection, not a confirmed tenant-history fact.
