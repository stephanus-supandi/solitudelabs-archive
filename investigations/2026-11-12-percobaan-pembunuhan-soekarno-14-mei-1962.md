## Article scope

This published historical article examines the attempted assassination of President Soekarno during the Eid al-Adha prayer at the Merdeka Palace on 14 May 1962. It compares the archival framing in an Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia (ANRI) YouTube publication with a popular-history video attributed in the supplied analysis to Portal Nusantara.

The article covers the historical event, the limits of what the ANRI video description establishes about its archival footage, competing reported names and roles associated with the attackers, the popular account of a shifting or doubled image of Soekarno, injuries among presidential guards and KH Zainul Arifin, the DI/TII context, Kartosuwiryo's capture and execution, and the development of presidential protection.

## Main evidence distinctions

- The ANRI video is identified in the supplied research as **“Upaya Pembunuhan Presiden Sukarno saat Sholat Idul Adha”**, published 17 June 2024 by the Arsip Nasional RI channel. Its description refers to footage of the atmosphere during the 1381 H Eid prayer at the Merdeka Palace. This does not by itself establish that the camera recorded the shooting itself.
- The second video is attributed in the supplied research to **Portal Nusantara**, with the title **“Kisah Ketika Bung Karno Ditembak Saat Salat Dari Jarak 7 Meter Tapi Meleset.”** Its exact metadata and full transcript were not independently retrieved during the archive operation. Treat its attribution and detailed narration as supplied research, not independently verified metadata.
- The event date is recorded as 14 May 1962 / 10 Dzulhijjah 1381 H, following the ANRI description cited in the article.
- Published accounts differ in how they identify the gunman and other participants. The article therefore does not collapse Sanusi, Bachrum, and other named individuals into one definitive role assignment without primary judicial or archival records.
- The “two images” / “moving shadow” account is preserved as a reported testimony or popular retelling, not as a demonstrated supernatural or physical fact.
- The relationship between the attack and DI/TII is discussed in historical context. A direct order from Kartosuwiryo to each attacker is not asserted without documentary evidence.
- Kartosuwiryo's capture and execution are included as contextual events; temporal proximity alone is not treated as proof of an operational command link.

## Open verification points

- The exact metadata and full contents of the Portal Nusantara video were not independently retrieved during the archive operation.
- The underlying original ANRI footage has not been examined frame by frame in this archive operation; the article relies on the published description for its characterization.
- Primary judicial records are needed to settle individual identities, roles, and any chain of command among the attackers.
- The “moving shadow” account requires tracing to its earliest identifiable testimony and checking it against the original source; it is not established as an objective physical event.
- The exact source wording and attribution for the reported seven-metre distance should be checked against the cited original publication or testimony.

## Method note

Separate archival description, surviving footage, contemporary reporting, later testimony, popular retelling, and editorial interpretation. A state archive's publication is important provenance, but the archive's authority does not automatically validate every inference made from the footage. Similar or repeated retellings are not independent corroboration unless their source chains are established.
