# SolitudeLabs Archive

An independent research and digital archive documenting urban archaeology, Indonesian history, power structures, human rights, technology, and society.

**Editorial principles**
- Evidence-first and source-traceable.
- Distinguish documented facts, reported information, inference, and unknowns.
- Preserve uncertainty; do not turn proximity, memory, or matching names into proof.
- Correct records when stronger evidence becomes available.

## Publication channels

SolitudeLabs articles are published on [WordPress](https://solitudelabs.wordpress.com/) and [Blogger](https://solitudelabs.blogspot.com/). This repository is the supporting archive and index; the original published articles remain canonical at their publication URLs.
