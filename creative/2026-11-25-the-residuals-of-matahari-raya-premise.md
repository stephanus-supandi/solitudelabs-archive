# The Residuals of Matahari Raya: The Premise

**Publication date:** 25 November 2026

## Concept

A grounded, atmospheric mystery / literary science-fiction thriller set in a layered, contemporary Bekasi. The protagonist, Raka, is a method-oriented structural analyst and experimental coder. A set of apparently routine anomalies—subtle ground settlement along a residential boulevard, an implausible GPS trajectory inside an old structure, and a rhythmic acoustic signal buried in noise—seem to converge on a forgotten pre-war site and a fragment of childhood memory.

## Core elements

- **Protagonist:** Raka, a structural analyst and systems tinkerer who trusts tolerances, boundary conditions, reproducible tests, and residuals.
- **Setting:** A humid, layered Bekasi, including newer housing estates, older neighborhood roads such as Jl. Matahari Raya, and historic landmarks.
- **Inciting anomaly:** Structural displacement appears inconsistent with the soil–structure interaction models tested. A synthetic consistency checker also flags a location trace that should not exist.
- **Central tension:** The pattern may indicate an engineering problem, deliberate concealment, or an overfitted interpretation shaped by memory and pattern recognition.
- **Narrative question:** What remains when every model is tightened but the residuals continue to point toward something personal?

## Proposed scene arc

1. **The Baseline:** A late-night walk establishes Raka's observational habits and methodical mindset.
2. **The Anomaly:** A crack pattern, GPS jump, or recurring signal cadence survives cleaning and re-testing.
3. **The Echo:** A mundane fragment of Raka's middle-school years becomes unexpectedly relevant.
4. **The Escalation:** Raka builds a dedicated tool to distinguish a real pattern from overfitting.
5. **The Turning Point:** In a half-forgotten structure, Raka must decide whether to publish, hand over, or leave the finding unresolved.

## Tone and style

Grounded, atmospheric, restrained, and slightly uncanny rather than melodramatic. Technical detail (stiffness, resonance, drift, signal-to-noise ratio, archival imagery, and residual analysis) should coexist with local texture and human memory.

## Evidence and fiction boundary

This is a **fictional premise**, not a report of a verified engineering failure, GPS anomaly, acoustic event, cover-up, or pre-war site. The use of real place names and a recognizable Bekasi setting does not establish that the described events occurred.
