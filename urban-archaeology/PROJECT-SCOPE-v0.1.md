# BLOON INVESTIGATION — URBAN & RURAL ENVIRONMENTAL ARCHIVE v0.1

**Status:** ARCHIVAL / EXPLORATORY  
**Adopted:** 4 November 2026  
**Repository:** `stephanus-supandi/solitudelabs-archive`

## 1. Purpose

This project documents and analyzes observable features of the built and rural environment across dated imagery and appropriately scoped supporting sources. Its subject matter includes streetscapes, buildings, signage, event banners, market structures, place naming, land-use context, and visible indicators of economic activity.

The archive is intended to preserve observations, source trails, uncertainty, corrections, and methodological limits. It is not a general-purpose investigation into private individuals.

## 2. Geographic scope

The current observational corpus contains:

- Bekasi, Indonesia
- Sorocaba, São Paulo, Brazil
- Jakarta Barat, Indonesia
- Bandar Lampung, Indonesia — urban subcorpora including Way Kandis and Yos Sudarso / Lampung City Mall
- Rural / residential Lampung material, retained only as privacy-protected environmental baseline where appropriate

The presence of a location in this list does not imply that every frame has a verified street-, district-, or city-level attribution. Geographic precision must not exceed the evidence supplied by the image or independent sources.

## 3. Research questions

The archive may examine:

1. What physical features, signs, and place markers are visibly present in a dated image?
2. Can a visible sign or event banner be correlated with independent, dated public documentation?
3. What changes can be observed when comparable imagery exists for the same location across different vintages?
4. Which map listings can be matched to visible signage, and what exactly does that match establish?
5. Which observations remain unresolved because of ambiguous names, conflicting addresses, insufficient image resolution, or missing source records?

A question must be stated before a batch is treated as a substantive investigative step. Repeated or duplicate frames do not create new findings.

## 4. Scope exclusions

This project is **not**:

- a legal audit of business entities, licenses, ownership, or tax status;
- a complete census of businesses or properties;
- proof of property ownership, tenancy, corporate control, or commercial operation;
- an investigation of private residents, customers, passers-by, or people named in map listings;
- a criminal investigation or an attempt to infer wrongdoing;
- evidence of a connection to another SolitudeLabs investigation unless a separately documented, testable link is established.

A map pin, category, rating, business card, or platform status is platform metadata. It is not, by itself, proof of historical physical presence or legal identity.

## 5. Evidence and status discipline

Claims must be atomic and assigned a status appropriate to their evidence. Use the established SolitudeLabs status vocabulary, including:

- **OBSERVED:** directly visible in the reviewed image, scoped to its vintage and frame.
- **OBSERVED-MATCH:** a platform listing and visible signage correspond at the observed location; this does not establish legal identity or ownership.
- **CORROBORATED:** supported by sufficiently independent sources.
- **REPORTED:** stated by a source but not independently established.
- **INFERENCE:** reasoned interpretation with its basis and alternatives stated.
- **UNVERIFIED / NOT VERIFIED:** insufficient evidence to establish the claim.
- **NOT RUN:** a specified test or search was not performed.
- **NOT FOUND IN REVIEWED RECORDS:** not found within an explicitly stated review scope; this does not prove non-existence.

Source quantity alone does not establish independence. Repeated screenshots, copied reports, and listings derived from the same underlying source must not be counted as independent corroboration.

## 6. Temporal rules

Imagery vintage is not necessarily the exact capture date. A dated event banner correlated with independent event reporting may identify the event and narrow the temporal context, but does not by itself establish whether the image was captured before, during, or after the event.

Change detection requires comparable evidence for the same location across distinct vintages. Different streets or panorama nodes must not be treated as a temporal sequence merely because their imagery dates differ.

## 7. Deduplication and frame registry

Every frame must receive a permanent registry ID and retain its original addendum/frame reference. Near-duplicates and duplicate-of-duplicate submissions are recorded as corpus-management events, not new evidence.

A repeated frame does not increase source count, confidence, or claim status. Locked frames remain locked when re-submitted.

Proposed registry pattern:

`BLOON-VIS-<CORPUS>-<NODE>-<VINTAGE>-<LISTING>-<ADDENDUM.FRAME>`

The registry must preserve the relationship between the permanent ID and the original submitted reference. Where node, vintage, or listing identity is uncertain, use a neutral identifier rather than inventing precision.

## 8. Privacy and safety

- Do not identify private individuals from imagery or map listings.
- Do not publish a personal name paired with a private residence.
- Do not infer residence, ownership, profession, or commercial activity from a personal-name listing.
- Do not deblur faces or license plates, perform face matching, or investigate private residents.
- Residential frames must not be geolocated more precisely than necessary for a legitimate, non-identifying environmental observation.
- Sensitive minority religious institutions associated with residential-looking buildings require heightened security review. Public verification should stop at an appropriately broad institutional level where precise publication could expose a private or vulnerable site.
- Locked material is excluded from public-facing images and identifying detail unless a documented safety review authorizes a narrower release.

## 9. Publication rules

Public outputs should distinguish observation from interpretation, disclose uncertainty, preserve correction history, and avoid publishing unnecessary personal or location-sensitive information.

Screenshots and source documents are not assumed to be present in the repository merely because an addendum describes them. Each uploaded artifact must state whether its underlying media, source bibliography, and metadata are actually included.
