## 2026-11-08

### Music industry · Asian music · Business research

- [88Rising: Bukan Sekadar Label Rekaman](investigations/2026-11-08-88rising-bukan-sekadar-label-rekaman.md)

Distinguishes sourced facts from business analysis and unverified contract, ownership, and financial details.

### Urban archaeology · Spatial memory · AI literacy

1. [BSK ke Kemang Pratama: Ingatan SMP, Google Maps, dan AI yang Terlalu Yakin](investigations/2026-11-08-bsk-kemang-pratama-ingatan-smp-google-maps-ai.md)  
   A BLOON investigation separating personal recollection, geographic plausibility, documented neighborhood history, and AI-generated reconstruction. Includes a supplementary methodological note on how human memory, maps, witnesses, and AI can correct one another without becoming unquestionable authorities.

2. [Laundry yang Hilang di Jl. Bintang Raya](investigations/2026-11-08-laundry-yang-hilang-di-jl-bintang-raya.md)  
   A micro-investigation into a remembered laundry business in Jaka Setia, Bekasi Selatan. Separates the existence of the street from the unverified existence and identity of the historical business; includes a post-publication audit cautioning against AI claims about multiple same-named streets and speculative reasons for closure.

## 2026-11-07

3. [Leak Bali — Antara Kutukan, Ilmu Pengleakan, Calon Arang, dan Mitologi Pulau Dewata](investigations/2026-11-07-leak-bali-mitologi-dan-sinema.md)

### Editorial · Research methodology

4. [Bam Bam Pamungkas — Ensiklopedia BLOON Internasional](investigations/2026-11-07-bam-bam-pamungkas-ensiklopedia-bloon.md)  
   A manifesto for Ensiklopedia BLOON as an independent editorial project, with evidence-status labels and rules for distinguishing facts, memories, hypotheses, and unknowns.

5. [Leak Bali Editorial Audit](research/2026-11-07-leak-bali-editorial-audit.md)  
   Post-publication audit preserving methodological cautions, a suggested cultural-context footnote, and QA findings. This is a research note, not a separate published article.

6. [Kalkulator Goblok — 2 + 2 = 4, Tetapi Siapa yang Menjamin Angka 2 Itu Benar?](investigations/2026-11-07-kalkulator-goblok-validasi-numerik.md)  
   A numerical-literacy and financial-research article using the Bank Ina case to distinguish mathematical correctness, rounded financial inputs, data provenance, and interpretation. The BLOON BANK CALCULATOR is presented as a proposal, not a completed or tested application.

7. [Kalkulator Goblok Editorial Audit](research/2026-11-07-kalkulator-goblok-editorial-audit.md)  
   A methodological audit clarifying the distinction between formal mathematical truth and empirical validation, with a four-layer validation model and notes on rounding, Decimal, and pytest. This is a research note, not a separate published article.
