# BLOON Street Observation — Makassar: Cendrawasih, Opu Daeng Risadju, and the Baji-* Streets

**Date recorded:** 2026-11-17  
**Coverage:** User-described screenshot batches 1–20 in the current investigation sequence  
**Status:** Working research dossier; based on the user's written descriptions of Google Maps / Street View screenshots. The screenshots were not independently re-inspected in this archive operation. No field survey, coordinate extraction, or independent listing verification was performed.

## Scope and evidence boundary

This dossier consolidates the first twenty batches described by the user as an evolving Makassar investigation. The recurring geographic leads include Jl. Opu Daeng Risadju, Jl. Cendrawasih, Jl. Andi Mappaodang, Jl. Dangkoe, Jl. Tanggul Patompo, and several streets or lanes beginning with “Baji”: Baji Dakka, Baji Maccini, Baji Passare, Baji Gio, Baji Sunggu, and Baji Nyawa. These labels suggest a Makassar-area street-network context, but their appearance across separate screenshot tiles does not establish one continuous route, exact adjacency, or a camera coordinate.

The material is a transcription of user-described visual and interface observations. It is not an independent image audit. POI names, categories, ratings, review counts, opening hours, dates, status-bar values, and icon colours are preserved as reported snapshots—not as current business facts.

## Repeated interface patterns and anomalies

### Selected-POI changes over apparently identical backgrounds

The user repeatedly described pairs or groups of screenshots in which the background scene appeared pixel-identical while the selected business panel or red POI pin changed. Examples reported include:

- Sentosa Motor / Pegadaian Unit Cendrawasih.
- Hanon Laptop / Greta Barbershop.
- Penjahit Mustika / Haji Muna / Ks Suku Jaya.
- Sentosa Motor and Super Cell Cendrawasih appearing again in later batches with different reported backgrounds.
- Sentosa Motor / Super Cell / CV Berkat Abadi / Kande mammi recurring across multiple batches.

If the backgrounds are in fact pixel-identical, this supports the limited observation that the same displayed background was paired with different selected POI states. It does not establish Google's internal rendering architecture, the source of the selected listing, or the geographic position of the camera.

### Same listing name across different poses

The user reported returning to Enduro Express, Zhengxin Chicken Steak, and Depot Air Minum Sumber in later batches with different visual poses. This is useful for separating a listing identity from a particular displayed scene. It does not prove that the listing's mapped coordinate is correct, that the camera was at the listing, or that the scenes form a continuous route.

### Category mismatches

Reported examples include:

- “Asrama mimun” categorized as “Museum · Open 24 hours.”
- “Apotik Sinar Jaya Sakkti” categorized as “Hospital,” with a reported 1.0 rating from 2 reviews.
- PDAM / Perusahaan Daerah Air Minum categorized as “Convenience store.”
- CV Garam Dunia categorized as “Sign shop.”
- Greta Barbershop categorized as “Pool hall.”
- Kande mammi categorized as “Asian restaurant.”

These are user-transcribed category mismatches. They show that the category displayed in a panel should not be treated as a direct visual observation or authoritative classification of the scene. The cause—wrong category, wrong entity match, stale data, or another interface issue—has not been established.

### Panel-name and physical-sign discrepancies

- “BCA Kantor Kas Cendrwasih” was reported in one Google panel, while another panel in the same batch spelled “Cendrawasih.”
- The physical sign was reported as “IKRAM,” while a panel elsewhere was described as “Iqram.”
- A PDAM listing panel appeared in tiles where “KOPI PENG” signage was also visible. Whether the coffee sign belongs to a separate adjacent business, a shared frontage, or the same complex is unresolved.

A spelling discrepancy is evidence of inconsistent displayed text, not proof that the underlying institution or street is misidentified.

### Icon and companion-glyph observations

The user reported several previously unseen or recurring glyphs: green pins, a purple/lavender building-like pin, a small red “H,” an orange coffee-cup icon, and a red star. Their meaning is not assigned here. Without a reliable legend or controlled comparison, colour and shape alone are insufficient to identify a category, status, cluster, or facility.

### Status-bar and date/header observations

The user described recurring combinations of a “Feb 2026” header, a status-bar clock that sometimes remained unchanged across several tiles and sometimes moved backward by one minute, and battery values that sometimes stayed flat or increased between screenshots. A separate Suno notification banner was reported in one screenshot.

These are distinct visual layers: map/viewer interface, Android status bar, and operating-system notification. Their appearance is recorded as reported, but no mechanism is inferred. In particular, a static clock or changing battery percentage does not by itself prove that a screenshot sequence was frozen, imported, stitched, or captured in one live session. Header dates and age labels also require interpretation from their own UI context.

## Working interpretation

The strongest current use of this material is as a set of geographic and listing leads around the Cendrawasih / Opu Daeng Risadju / Baji-* street-name cluster in Makassar. Institutional names (for example Pegadaian, BRI, BCA, Panin, Puskesmas, and PDAM) and physical street/business signs provide useful targets for independent address checking.

The following distinctions remain mandatory:

1. **City or district context** is not the same as a verified street segment.
2. **A street-name label or physical sign** is not the same as an exact camera coordinate.
3. **A POI name, rating, category, or panel** is not proof that the camera stood at that business.
4. **A physical object or building** may be visible from multiple camera positions.
5. **Repeated names and visual co-presence** do not establish ownership, tenancy, business relationships, or continuity.
6. **A UI anomaly** does not establish the software mechanism that produced it.

No camera coordinates have been supplied or independently derived in this dossier. The exact camera positions therefore remain **unverified**, not proven nonexistent or inherently unverifiable.

## Method note

This dossier preserves user-described observations and deliberately avoids converting repeated labels into a route or exact camera location. No normalized coordinate rows are added to a BEO/GSV dataset. No current business status, institutional address, rating, category, opening hour, or legal identity is independently verified here.
