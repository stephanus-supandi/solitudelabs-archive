## Cross-batch findings and evidence discipline

### Geographic hypotheses

- **Pontianak:** Strong working hypothesis for the batch 23–32 sequence, supported by reported physical school/banner text, local political-district wording, a UGC caption, and later POI labels. Not every frame is independently geolocated, and the exact camera positions remain unverified.
- **Jakarta Utara / Tanjung Priok:** Strong working hypothesis for batches 33–34, supported by the reported Pos IX Satlantas physical sign naming Jakarta Utara and the Pos 9 Pelabuhan Tg. Priok POI label. The exact panorama camera coordinates and full route remain unverified.
- **Dangko / Tanggul Patompo:** Batch 35 provides a possible return to a previously reported street-name corridor. The apparent connection to earlier Makassar material is a lead, not an independently matched route.
- **Bird-name streets:** Merpati, Gelatik, Camar, and Cendrawasih form a partial naming layer; “Gg. Family” shows it is not exclusive. “Gg. Gemar” remains ambiguous and is not automatically included.

### Repeated structural patterns

- **One facade/building, multiple selected POIs:** Susan Salon's multi-layer name/address agreement; two listings on the Al-Falah complex; the reported Musholla / Tukang Gigi cross-batch match; and the Ayam Geprek / Aurora Beauty Studio toggle pair. Each case needs its own evidence and should not be generalized into a universal database rule.
- **POI category mismatches:** TK sign versus IBabytopia pin; Pos IX Satlantas versus “Traffic officer”; and print shop versus “Corporate office,” among earlier category anomalies. These show that displayed category fields can diverge from the user's description of visible premises; they do not explain the underlying classification process.
- **Aggregate panels:** Review counts, ratings, and review-summary snippets are metadata snapshots, not direct evidence of location, quality, or camera position.
- **Temporal packages:** A displayed imagery epoch can coincide with changed scene content (batch 30), show no demonstrated content delta (batch 32), or permit a same-intersection comparison across dates (batch 33). None automatically proves a social, political, or construction change.
- **Date layers:** Header dates, “years ago” text, watermarks, and Android status-bar clocks/battery readings are distinct UI/display layers. Inconsistency is observable; import, stitching, freezing, or capture-session mechanisms remain unknown without source evidence.
- **Occlusion / missing scene:** Batch 29 f4 is not treated as a readable scene where the smear blocks the relevant pixels. Its cause remains unresolved.
- **Medium boundaries:** A panorama, vector map, UGC photo, and 3D volumetric map do not share the same geometry or camera semantics. A 3D view has no panorama camera pose; an empty photo placeholder does not establish global photo absence.

## Method note

This is a working dossier based on the user's written descriptions, not independent screenshot inspection. Keep physical signs, map/POI labels, UGC captions, review aggregates, historical-imagery panels, watermarks, status-bar chrome, and camera poses separate. City-level context is not a camera coordinate. Repeated labels or apparent scene matches do not by themselves establish continuous route, exact pin placement, ownership, tenancy, legal identity, or capture provenance. Do not infer construction progress from perspective differences. Do not infer the mechanism behind date-layer conflicts or scene smears. No normalized coordinates, official-register checks, field observations, or BEO/GSV coordinate rows are added in this update.
