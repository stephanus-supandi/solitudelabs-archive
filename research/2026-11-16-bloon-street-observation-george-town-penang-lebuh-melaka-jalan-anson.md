2. **Kong Jee Trading:** the Maps category “financial institution” does not establish that it is a bank or money changer.

4. **Klinik Loo:** a 1.9-star rating does not establish poor clinical quality, patient harm, inadequate facilities, or failure to adapt. No review text was inspected in this archive operation.
5. **Premium appliance retail:** displayed brands do not establish customer wealth, target-income brackets, sales volume, or the socioeconomic character of the surrounding area.
6. **Anson Cube:** a modern building near older facades does not by itself prove gentrification, heritage conflict, or development pressure.
7. **Kopitiam / UNESCO:** the presence of a kopitiam does not establish that this specific business or building is individually UNESCO-listed or legally protected as intangible heritage.
8. **Tsurutami / KTV / barber / trophy shop:** names and signs support leads for business classification, not verified hours, current operation, history, or cultural significance.
9. **Visual adjacency:** neighboring signs, shared-looking fences, plants, facades, and background structures do not establish a shared legal address, tenant relationship, ownership, or single commercial complex.
10. **POI totals:** the user-provided summaries variously state approximately 15, 20, 25, 30, and 35 Penang POIs. These totals are not a stable unique-POI count; revisits and repeated listings may be included. No definitive total is asserted.
11. **Cross-country comparisons:** reported Bekasi, Jakarta, Georgia, and Penang totals and ratings were not re-audited in this operation. Do not claim a statistically comparable four-country dataset, global champion, or economic ranking from the supplied summary tables.
12. **UNESCO boundary:** George Town's World Heritage status does not automatically prove that every POI or facade in these observations lies inside the inscribed property or buffer zone.
