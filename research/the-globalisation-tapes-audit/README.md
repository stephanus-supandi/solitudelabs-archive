## Purpose

This folder separates source-backed facts from unresolved identity, contact, and funding questions concerning *The Globalisation Tapes* (2003), IUF, SERBUK, Hemasari Dharmabumi, and the later research trajectory toward *The Act of Killing*.

## Files

- `source-register.csv` — identified sources, what each supports, and limitations.
- `people.csv` — people explicitly named in the reviewed sources.
- `organizations.csv` — organizations and their documented roles.
- `relationships.csv` — claim-level edges with evidence status.
- `chronology.md` — dated sequence and source caveats.
- `unresolved-questions.md` — questions requiring additional primary records.
- `relationship-map.mmd` — conservative relationship diagram.

## Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| VERIFIED | Directly supported by an identified source within the stated scope. |
| CORROBORATED | Supported by more than one independent source. |
| REPORTED | Stated by a secondary source; not independently established here. |
| INFERENCE | Interpretation, not a directly documented fact. |
| UNKNOWN | Available evidence does not answer the question. |
| NOT VERIFIED | The claim has not been adequately established. |

## Guardrails

1. Do not identify the unnamed female IUF labour educator as Hemasari without direct evidence.
2. Do not infer a direct Hemasari–Oppenheimer communication edge from job title or institutional proximity.
3. Do not treat APHEDA support for the Labour Education Centre as proof of funding for the film.
4. Do not merge *The Globalisation Tapes* and *The Act of Killing* funding streams.
5. Preserve the dissertation's G Siahaan / R. Siahaan spelling discrepancy.
6. Do not treat the twelve names in footnote 52 as a verified complete membership roll of SERBUK.
7. Distinguish the five Dramatis Personae entries from the twelve-person footnote list.
