## Cross-medium observations

The user characterized the sequence as moving among panorama, vector map, and user-contributed imagery. This is a useful working distinction, but each medium has a different evidence structure:

| Medium | What may be observed | What it does not establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Street View / panorama | Visible scene, road furniture, buildings, imagery-date labels, camera perspective | Exact coordinate without a reliable match to fixed landmarks or source metadata |
| 2D vector map | POI pins, labels, search terms, administrative strings, categories | A physical camera position or proof that the selected pin is correctly placed |
| User-contributed photograph | Visible objects, contributor attribution as displayed, contribution/date panel text | Photographer's exact standing point, capture date unless specified, or exact terminal identity |

A real ship, quay, or gantry crane is a physical object. Its visibility does not alone identify the camera position. Conversely, lack of a coordinate in the screenshot description does not mean the photograph is impossible to geolocate. Fixed terminal structures, vessel identity, source metadata, and independently documented port layouts may provide verification paths if the original image and sufficient detail become available.
