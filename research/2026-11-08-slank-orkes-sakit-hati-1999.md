# Slank — “Orkes Sakit Hati” (1999): Lagu Populer dan Harapan kepada Pejabat Terpilih

**Status:** supporting research note; not a standalone published article  
**Prepared:** 2026-11-08  
**Related article:** *BLOON INVESTIGATION — INDONESIA 1998–2009: DARI SHEILA ON 7, PETERPAN, F4, SAMPAI WALI*  
**Scope:** a source-backed addition for the Indonesian popular-music timeline, not a replacement for the full article.

## Verified points

1. Slank's official discography dates the studio album **`9.999+09 Hitam`** to **1 October 1999** and identifies **“Orkes Sakit Hati”** and **“Ngangkang”** as its hit singles. The official discography also lists the album's personnel and production information.
2. ANTARA's 10 September 2024 report says the song's music was initially composed by Kaka without lyrics; Bimbim added lyrics in June 1999. ANTARA describes the lyrics as expressing hope toward officials elected in the 1999 general election.
3. The song's recurring themes—broken promises, trust, responsibility, and freedom—make it a useful example of popular music carrying a civic or political message in the Reformasi period.

## Interpretation boundary

The song is evidence that Slank's music could engage with the public mood around the 1999 election. It is **not**, by itself, evidence of the song's reception among all voters, a measurable political impact, or the position of every Indonesian musician at the time. The 2024 ANTARA article is a retrospective report; it should be cited as such rather than presented as a contemporaneous 1999 news report.

For the main 1998–2009 music article, place Slank alongside—not instead of—the other artists and bands discussed. The addition broadens the picture beyond romance, youth culture, and the commercial music industry by including a clear example of civic commentary in a popular song.
