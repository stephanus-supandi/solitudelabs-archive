# BLOON INVESTIGATION — Leak Bali Editorial Audit

- **Prepared:** 2026-11-07
- **Related publication:** [Leak Bali — Antara Kutukan, Ilmu Pengleakan, Calon Arang, dan Mitologi Pulau Dewata](../investigations/2026-11-07-leak-bali-mitologi-dan-sinema.md)

## Purpose

This note preserves the post-publication audit of the Leak Bali article and separates supported cultural discussion from interpretive extensions and unsupported generalizations.

## Audit findings

| Statement | Assessment | Editorial handling |
|---|---|---|
| Leak is more than the horror-film image of a flying head | Supported as a broad corrective | Explain the range of cultural and popular representations. |
| Leak is an ilmu, not a ghost | Too absolute | Avoid a single universal definition; identify the source and context. |
| Pengleakan is a neutral tool | Analogy, not established fact | Label as an analogy or omit. |
| Pangiwa is evil and Panengen is good | Over-simplified | Describe source-specific meanings; avoid universal binary claims. |
| Calon Arang can be read through stigma and gender | Valid modern interpretation | Identify it as one interpretive lens, not the sole meaning. |
| Rangda and Leak are identical | Not supported as a blanket statement | Keep distinctions and explain the specific performance/text context. |
| Barong and Rangda are simply equal opposites or twins | Overgeneralization | Require specific cultural scholarship before making a cosmological claim. |
| Film imagery can feed back into popular folklore | Discussed in Frolova (2026) | Attribute the concept and do not generalize beyond the study. |
| Leak has no physical evidence | Incorrectly broad formulation | Say that supernatural claims have not been empirically verified. |

## Editorial guardrails

1. Respect Balinese cultural traditions while distinguishing belief, text, performance, social effects, and empirical claims.
2. Never identify a living person as Leak or repeat a local accusation as fact without reliable evidence and careful ethical/legal review.
3. Do not use a film as the sole source for traditional belief.
4. Do not convert an analogy into ethnographic evidence.
5. Do not turn one literary interpretation into the definitive meaning of a narrative.
6. Do not infer a single pan-Balinese system from one regional or institutional source.
7. Do not connect unrelated SolitudeLabs articles merely because they share a pattern or metaphor.

## Suggested publication footnote

> Artikel ini membahas Leak sebagai fenomena folklor, kepercayaan, sastra, dan representasi budaya Bali. Istilah Leak dan ilmu pengleakan tidak diperlakukan sebagai satu definisi tunggal yang berlaku untuk seluruh masyarakat Bali. Pemaknaan dapat berbeda menurut sumber, wilayah, tradisi, konteks ritual, dan perkembangan budaya populer.
>
> Tokoh Calon Arang, Empu Bharadah, serta representasi Rangda dibahas berdasarkan tradisi sastra dan pertunjukan. Penafsiran Calon Arang sebagai korban stigma sosial merupakan salah satu pendekatan pembacaan modern, bukan satu-satunya tafsir.
>
> Pembahasan mengenai *reverse involution* merujuk pada kajian tentang hubungan antara representasi sinema dan imajinasi folklor. Hal tersebut tidak berarti seluruh gambaran Leak dalam masyarakat Bali berasal dari film.
>
> Artikel ini tidak membuktikan ataupun menyangkal keberadaan kekuatan supernatural. Klaim supernatural yang belum memiliki bukti empiris terverifikasi tidak boleh ditulis sebagai fakta ilmiah.
>
> **Menghormati tradisi bukan berarti mengubah kepercayaan menjadi fakta ilmiah. Mengkritisi klaim bukan berarti merendahkan kebudayaan.**
