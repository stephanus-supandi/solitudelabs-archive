## Summary

Catatan nostalgia tentang seorang teman di Jalan Batu Mulia II, Jaka Mulya, Bekasi Selatan, yang mengajari penulis bermain gitar dan memperkenalkannya pada gitar instrumental serta nama-nama seperti Satriani, Vai, Petrucci, Gambale, Govan, Holdsworth, dan Paul Gilbert. Menelusuri hubungan Paul Gilbert dengan Guitar Institute of Technology (GIT) di Musicians Institute, serta rekam jejak Bernard B2n melalui katalog Gramedia dan wawancara 2011: pendidikan di GIT, album Searchin’ for The Dream dan Helping Hands, buku gitar, serta keterlibatannya di Institut Musisi Indonesia (IMI). Artikel juga membedakan Musicians Institute, Institut Musisi Indonesia, dan Ikatan Motor Indonesia; mencatat perubahan IMI menjadi Sekolah Tinggi Musik Basuki Indonesia pada 2013; dan menempatkan cerita teman di Jaka Mulya sebagai ingatan pribadi yang belum diverifikasi secara independen.

## Evidence distinctions

### Publicly traceable information
- Musicians Institute's Paul Gilbert success-story page records his move to Los Angeles in 1984 to study at MI, then known for its guitar program as Guitar Institute of Technology, and his later teaching role.
- Gramedia Pustaka Utama's catalog entry for *Teknik Mahir Gitar Elektrik* identifies Bernard B2n as a GIT graduate, records his teaching and Director of Education role at Institut Musisi Indonesia, and lists the albums *Searchin’ for The Dream* (fusion rock) and *Helping Hands* (fusion jazz).
- Gramedia's catalog also lists Bernard B2n's *Fusion Guitar* (2008).
- A 2011 interview reproduced by GUTARISAMIENS records Bernard's account of studying at GIT in 1995, returning to Indonesia in September 1996, and his involvement in the formation of IMI.
- The article cites institutional history describing IMI's founding in 2001 and its later status change to Sekolah Tinggi Musik Basuki Indonesia in 2013.

### Personal recollection
- The author remembers a guitar-playing friend living on Jalan Batu Mulia II, Jaka Mulya, Bekasi Selatan.
- The friend taught the author guitar and introduced him to instrumental guitarists.
- The author recalls that the friend eventually studied at Institut Kesenian Jakarta (IKJ), and that a Bernard B2n cassette—possibly *Searchin’ for The Dream*—was heard at the time.

### Unverified details
- The friend's identity and educational history have not been independently verified.
- The exact nature and timing of any plan by the friend to study at Musicians Institute remain based on memory.
- The cassette title is tentative; the article does not establish that the cassette heard was definitely *Searchin’ for The Dream*.
- The public record about Bernard B2n does not independently establish a direct personal connection between Bernard and the author's friend.
